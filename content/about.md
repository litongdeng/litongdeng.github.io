Hi! I’m Litong, a junior at UC Berkeley double majoring in applied mathematics and public health, with a minor in journalism. I’m currently a Dow Jones News Fund multiplatform editing intern at the San Francisco Chronicle and a deputy night editor at The Daily Californian.

I’m especially interested in science journalism. I've interviewed Fields medalists, MacArthur Fellows and many distinguished researchers and professors from around the world as I aim to break down and communicate complex scientific ideas to bring more science to more people.
